## Who Relay serves

- Researchers and engineers building multi-model orchestration pipelines
- Product teams who need consistent multi-LLM orchestration with tenant isolation
- Academic collaborators and paper reproducibility workflows

## Cost / Performance value prop

- Performant: routes simple queries to low-cost models and fans out to better models only for complex tasks
- Cost-aware: per-query budgeting and per-session tracking to bound spend
- Latency-controlled: budget/latency tradeoffs exposed to clients

## User journeys

1. Developer: registers tenant keys in the dashboard, runs local dev, integrates web client.
2. Researcher: runs benchmark pipelines via ml_pipeline to compare model mixtures.
3. Product owner: sets budget/latency policies and inspects model trust metrics.
